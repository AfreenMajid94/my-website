FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is Bookkaru?

Bookkaru is Pakistan's leading e-ticketing platform, offering a fast and simple way to book bus, train, flight, and event e-tickets. Our app provides a seamless booking experience, allowing you to plan your trips and events with just a few clicks.

How do I create an account on Bookkaru?

Creating an account on Bookkaru is simple! Click on the "Sign up" button in the top right corner, fill in your details including username, email address, phone number, and password. Once you confirm your account, you can start booking tickets immediately.

How can I book a ticket using Bookkaru?

Booking a ticket on Bookkaru is quick and easy. Simply select your preferred service (Bus, Flight, Car, Event, Cinema, Attractions, or Tours), enter your journey details including from, to, and date, then click search. Choose from the available options and complete your payment to confirm your booking.

What payment methods are accepted on Bookkaru?

Bookkaru accepts multiple payment methods including credit/debit cards, bank transfers, jazzcash, easypaisa, and other digital payment options. All transactions are secure and encrypted for your safety.

Can I cancel or modify my booking?

Yes, you can cancel or modify your booking through your Bookkaru account. Please note that cancellation policies may vary depending on the service provider and the time of cancellation. You can find detailed information in your booking confirmation.

How do I receive my e-ticket?

After completing your booking, you will receive your e-ticket via email and SMS. You can also view and download your e-ticket from your Bookkaru account under "My Bookings". Simply present the e-ticket at the counter or scan it at the entry point.
